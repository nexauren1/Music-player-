# Music Player — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 6, 2026

Music Player (Android package: `com.nexauren.musicplayer2`) is an offline-first music player developed by **Nexauren**.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use Music Player, why it is processed, and your choices.

## 1. Information stored on your device

Music Player can access music and video media that you choose to make available to the app through Android media permissions.

This may include local media metadata such as:

- Song, artist, album, genre, duration, and file information.
- Playlists, favorites, playback settings, equalizer settings, and other app preferences.

Your local music files are not uploaded to Nexauren servers for normal playback. The app is designed to play media stored on your device.

## 2. Accounts and Firebase

Some optional features use Firebase services from Google.

When you create or use an account, Music Player may process:

- Email address.
- Display name.
- Firebase user ID (UID).
- Sign-in provider, such as email/password or Google.
- Account and authentication events.
- Premium entitlement information associated with your account.

Premium entitlement data may include the selected plan, verification status, expiration information, order ID, and subscription ID.

Firebase processes this information on behalf of the application. Firebase may also process technical information required to provide its services. See Google's Firebase privacy information for more details.

## 3. Analytics

Music Player uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the application is used and to improve reliability and features.

The app sends events such as:

- App session start and end.
- Screen views and time spent on screens.
- Sign-in and sign-up events and the sign-in method.
- Profile changes and account deletion.
- Premium entitlement synchronization and related errors.

Firebase Analytics can also automatically collect certain app-usage and technical information according to Google's Firebase Analytics behavior and configuration.

Music Player does not intentionally send the contents of your local music files to Analytics.

## 4. Premium payments

Music Player offers optional Premium features through PayPal.

When you start or verify a Premium purchase, the application may send your Firebase UID, selected plan, order ID, or subscription ID to the Music Player payment verification service.

PayPal processes payment and financial information under PayPal's own privacy policy. Music Player does not receive or store your full card or bank details.

The Music Player backend uses Cloudflare Workers to communicate with PayPal and to verify Premium entitlements.

## 5. App updates

Music Player may contact GitHub-hosted update resources to check for application updates and download release files.

These requests may be associated with normal network information such as your IP address by the service provider handling the request. Music Player does not use the update service to upload your personal music library.

## 6. Third-party services

Depending on the features you use, information may be processed by:

- **Google Firebase** — authentication, analytics, and Firestore entitlement data.
- **Google** — Google account sign-in when you choose Google authentication.
- **PayPal** — payment processing and transaction services.
- **Cloudflare** — operation of the payment verification backend.
- **GitHub** — application update files and release resources.

Each third-party provider has its own privacy and data-handling practices.

## 7. Permissions

Music Player requests permissions only for features that require them, including media access, background audio playback, notifications, network access, and application update installation where supported by Android.

Music Player does not request Android precise location permission.

## 8. Data retention and deletion

Local app data normally remains on your device until you remove it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the application.

You can sign out of a Music Player account and use the account deletion feature provided by the app.

If you need a privacy request concerning cloud data or a deletion request that is not fully handled by the app, contact Nexauren through the developer contact provided on the application store listing.

Third-party services may retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations.

## 9. Security

Nexauren uses reasonable technical measures to protect information processed by the application and its backend services, including encrypted network connections and server-side handling of payment credentials.

No Internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 10. Children's privacy

Music Player is not directed to children under 13. Nexauren does not knowingly request or collect personal information from children under 13 through the application.

## 11. International processing

Because Music Player uses third-party services such as Google, PayPal, Cloudflare, and GitHub, information may be processed in countries other than your country of residence, subject to the providers' policies and applicable law.

## 12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application, its services, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
